How to tell them apart

  • parser: the body shows day and month swapped (7 April → 4 July). The tenant writes the day first and the report came by app or email, or by form on booking-v2.
  • hint: the same swap, but sent through the booking-v3 form by a day-first tenant. booking-v3 prints MM/DD/YYYY under the date field, people type what it says, and the form reads it the tenant’s way. The parser fix does not touch this.
  • shift: no swap at all. The date moves by one day (Tuesday 12 May → Monday 11 May), differs between clients in Lisbon and Toronto, or the invite arrives before the booking. That is a time-zone conversion, not date order. These are the one-day moves in desk-notes.json. New ones belong in #3, not in a closed #4471.
