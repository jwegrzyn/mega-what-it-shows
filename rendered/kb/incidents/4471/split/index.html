How to tell them apart

  • parser: the body shows day and month swapped (7 April → 4 July). The tenant writes the day first and the report came by app or email, or by form on booking-v2. Fix: the shared parser reads dates in the tenant's locale.
  • hint: the same swap, but sent through the booking-v3 form by a day-first tenant (alder.physio, copper.studio, pale.harbour). booking-v3 prints MM/DD/YYYY under the date field, people type what it says, and the form reads it the tenant's way. The parser fix does not touch this: the hint must follow the tenant's locale.
  • shift: no swap at all. The date moves by one day (Tuesday 12 May → Monday 11 May), differs between clients in Lisbon and Toronto, or the invite arrives before the booking. That is a time-zone conversion, not date order. These are the reports listed in desk-notes.json as one-day moves; they were footnoted under parser, so they had no ticket.

Why it was merged as one

  • Intake tags by the reporter's browser, not the tenant. Every report from an en-US browser was tagged hint and every other one parser, so the tags miss both real groups (see the "intake tagged" column).
  • Decision 0001 merges on subject. These reports have twelve different subjects and still landed in one ticket, and the subject says nothing about the cause. Merge on cause: tenant locale, booking form, channel, and what the body shows.
  • The export repeats reports across overlapping pages. Count by id; the table does.
